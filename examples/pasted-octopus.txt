Title: 3 Octopus Facts That Sound Fake (But Are Real)

[0–3s] Hook
Visual: Close-up of an octopus eye staring at the camera.
On-screen text: "This animal is basically an alien 👽"
Voiceover: "This animal is so weird, scientists still can't fully explain it."

[3–10s] Fact 1
Visual: Octopus swimming, then a simple animation of three glowing hearts.
On-screen text: "3 HEARTS ❤️❤️❤️"
Voiceover: "First, an octopus has three hearts. Two pump blood to the gills, and one pumps it to the rest of the body."

[10–17s] Fact 2
Visual: Animation of blood turning blue.
On-screen text: "BLUE BLOOD 💙"
Voiceover: "And its blood is blue, because it uses copper instead of iron to carry oxygen."

[17–25s] Fact 3
Visual: Arms moving and grabbing objects, with "brain" icons appearing on each arm.
On-screen text: "ARMS THAT THINK 🧠"
Voiceover: "But the craziest part? Most of its brain cells are in its arms. Each arm can taste, touch, and react almost on its own."

[25–30s] Outro / CTA
Visual: Octopus disappearing by camouflaging into a rock.
On-screen text: "Which animal next? 👇"
Voiceover: "Three hearts, blue blood, and arms that think. Comment which animal I should do next!"

Production tips:

Use tense, mysterious background music that builds toward fact 3.
Cut every 2–3 seconds to keep retention high.
The camouflage ending works as a satisfying "disappearing" loop, which encourages rewatches.
Free octopus footage is available on stock sites like Pexels and Pixabay.
