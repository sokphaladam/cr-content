Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio · Effective 13 October 2026

Shorts Studio is a desktop application for Windows that helps you write, edit and render short vertical videos, and optionally upload them to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Shorts Studio runs on your computer. The developer runs no server for it and receives none of your data.
  • Your videos, drafts, keys and sign-in tokens are stored only in folders on your computer that you choose.
  • Information leaves your computer only when you use a feature that calls a third-party service, such as uploading a video to TikTok.
  • We do not sell, share or use your information for advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your computer

The app keeps the following in its data folder (chosen in Settings → Storage):

  • Scripts, drafts, uploaded media, rendered videos and thumbnails you create.
  • API keys you enter for optional services.
  • Sign-in tokens for accounts you connect (YouTube, TikTok), and the account display name and channel ID used to show which account is connected.
  • Your upload schedule and a record of which videos were uploaded where.

You can delete any of it at any time by disconnecting an account in Settings or deleting the files.

TikTok

If you connect a TikTok account, Shorts Studio uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, with your permission, to:

  • read your basic profile information (display name and username), to show the connected account (user.info.basic);
  • upload videos you choose, with the caption and privacy setting you choose, to your account or to your TikTok inbox as a draft (video.upload, video.publish).

The app only uploads videos when you ask it to, or at times you schedule. It does not read your other videos, followers, messages or any other TikTok data. Access tokens are stored only on your computer, and you can revoke access at any time by disconnecting in Settings or from your TikTok account's security settings. TikTok's handling of data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

YouTube

If you connect a YouTube channel, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload the videos you choose and, if you enable it, post a comment on them. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at Google security settings.

Other services you can turn on

When you use these features, the text or media needed for that feature is sent directly from your computer to the service, under that service's own privacy policy:

  • Anthropic (Claude): writing scripts, titles and descriptions from your topic.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech: turning narration into a voiceover.
  • Pixabay and Pexels: searching stock footage.
  • ElevenLabs: composing music; Jamendo: searching background music.

Children

Shorts Studio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
